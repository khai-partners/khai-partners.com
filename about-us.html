About Us

Khai & Partners Law Firm
Firm Overview

KHAI & Partners is an Indonesian law firm headquartered in Balikpapan, East Kalimantan, founded on a premise we have never found reason to complicate: that legal counsel, properly practised, is an exercise in applied justice — the patient translation of doctrine and procedure into outcomes that withstand scrutiny, protect interest, and endure long after the matter itself has closed.

We advise individuals, enterprises, and institutions across fifteen areas of practice, spanning litigation and alternative dispute resolution, corporate and commercial law, insolvency, employment, property, administrative and regulatory affairs, civil law, and intellectual property.

Our method is disciplined rather than transactional. Every engagement opens with rigorous factual and doctrinal analysis, proceeds through a strategy calibrated not to the maximal claim but to the client's actual objective, and closes with a result our partners are prepared to stand behind personally — in writing, in court, and in conversation.

What distinguishes KHAI & Partners is not size but structure. Every matter is led directly by a partner, from the first reading of the file to its final resolution — never delegated downward, never reviewed only in hindsight.

We regard this as a matter of professional responsibility as much as of service quality. The advocate whose name appears on the pleading should be the advocate who has read every page of it.

Our Approach

Our method follows a deliberate sequence, not a fixed formula. It begins with exhaustive factual and legal analysis — the discipline of understanding a matter completely before presuming to characterise it. It proceeds to a strategy calibrated to the client's true objective, which is not always identical to the maximal claim the law permits. Execution follows with precision: drafting, negotiation, mediation, or litigation, each pursued with the same evidentiary rigour, whatever the forum. And because a legal matter is rarely concluded neatly at judgment or at signature, we remain engaged through implementation, and, where it matters, through the longer consequences a decision carries into a client's affairs.

From the facts to a lasting resolution

Every engagement runs in sequence. Our opinions always rest on the applicable statute, regulation and case law.

  1. 01

    Factual & legal analysis

    We test the facts and evidence, then map the provisions of law that genuinely govern your matter.

  2. 02

    Strategy aimed at your objective

    Litigation, negotiation, mediation or arbitration, chosen for your real objective, not by habit.

  3. 03

    Careful execution

    Documents, arguments and procedure are prepared with precision, because procedure rewards it.

  4. 04

    Through to implementation

    We stay involved until the judgment or agreement can actually be enforced and carried out.

A judgment that cannot be enforced has limited practical value, so we plan for enforcement from the very start.

Why KHAI & Partners

Prospective clients are entitled to ask what distinguishes one firm from another. We offer the following, stated plainly:

Direct Partner Engagement

Every matter is carried personally by a partner from intake to resolution. It is never consigned downward and supervised at a distance.

Doctrinal Rigor

Every opinion we render rests on the applicable statute, regulation, and jurisprudence. We do not advise by approximation.

Candour

We tell clients what the law permits, and what it does not, including when the answer is unwelcome. The Indonesian Advocate's Code of Ethics requires this candour of us; we regard it as a professional obligation, not a constraint to be managed.

Breadth Without Dilution

Fifteen practice areas under a single roof, each overseen by a partner who has done the doctrinal work that competent advice in it demands.

A Firm Built Deliberately, and Described Honestly

We do not claim an institutional history longer than the one we have. What we offer instead is the direct and accountable attention of the partners whose names are on the door — and who intend to keep it that way.

Get In Touch

KHAI & Partners was established to practise law as its partners believe it ought to be practised: with rigour, candour, and direct accountability to the client. We welcome the opportunity to discuss a matter at whatever stage it has reached — however preliminary, however pressing.
