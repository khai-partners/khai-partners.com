Job Requirements

We welcome legal professionals who bring sound judgment, disciplined preparation, and a commitment to professional integrity. The role involves legal research, drafting, client communication, and support across contentious and non-contentious matters.

  • Bachelor's degree in Law (S.H.) from an accredited university.
  • Advocate qualification and completion of the advocate's oath are preferred. Please indicate your current qualification status in your application.
  • Experience in legal practice, particularly litigation, corporate matters, or dispute resolution, is an advantage.
  • Strong legal research, analytical, and drafting skills, with careful attention to facts and supporting evidence.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication in Indonesian. Proficiency in English is an advantage.
  • Ability to prepare legal opinions, agreements, pleadings, and professional correspondence clearly and accurately.
  • Strong professional ethics, discretion, and respect for client confidentiality.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively, manage deadlines, and respond constructively to feedback.
  • Willingness to work in Balikpapan, East Kalimantan, and travel when required for professional assignments.

Please submit your CV in PDF format through the application form and enter “Lawyer Application” in the Subject field. In the About Me section, briefly describe your experience, advocate qualification status, areas of interest, and motivation for joining KHAI & Partners.
